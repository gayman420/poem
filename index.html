A Poem

M.

for Maham

SCROLL↓

Would you run to me

My sweet M.

Where would you go

Take me with you

Your eyes, they belong to me

My words, they exist for you

Our hearts, they ache in sync

Your hair in the wind

I beg you take me with you

wherever you're going

i see no purpose

without your soul

without your smile

the way you touch my hands

the way you nod

give yourself to me

welcome me

look at me

tell me i am the only one

tell me forever

i can feel myself, getting lost in your embrace

do you live for me, will you die for me

tell me you belong to me

my daydreams are for you alone

tell me M.

will we flourish

even the clouds are weeping

the rain slides off my petals

the wind drags me to you

do the thorns in my stem hurt you

i will rip them out for you

i will be born anew

M. do you call it a curse

i will kiss it away

i will kiss your scars

with these lips

yours alone

take me with you, my darling

tell me the world through your eyes

is it meant to be so empty

i dont understand

but you make sense

let me inside

open the door

please, its cold

i dont know

is it a crime to be unsure

why would it be

to be afraid

of what comes next

when the web of destiny

knits a fate so cruel for us

do you not want to

talk about anything

then rest in my arms

and weep

take me with you M.

take me to where you go

that world i'd like to see

just once

and maybe i wont return

could i see the stars the way you do

fix me, ruin me, i dont care

make me yours

paint me in a color you love

kill me in the way you'd want

devour every part of me

and make it yours

M. get through it all

like you always do

with the strength of a thousand soldiers

and know that back home

im waiting for you

a thousand years

find your way back home

rest assured

i wait for you

take me with you, my dear M.

please

this world is nothing to me

without you in it

kiss me, kill me

love me, hurt me

hold me, as tight as you want

what could i want more

run to me

my sweet M.

wherever you go

take me with you

— always
